Generate 8 mascot design options for "Yappity", a free Windows voice typing app. You press a shortcut, talk ("yap"), and your words get typed for you. The vibe is playful and a little funny: you can ramble and it comes out clean.

Make 8 clearly different concepts so the owner can pick one. Mix it up and surprise us, for example a cute anime character, cute animals (a parrot, a hamster, a cat, a duck...), a little blob creature, a talking mic or speech bubble character. Each concept should feel friendly and memorable and work as a small app icon too.

Rules for every image:
- one character only, centered, full body, cheerful pose that hints at talking or yapping
- square 1024x1024, plain white background, no text, no letters, no logos
- clean modern illustration, bold simple shapes, soft shading
- use purple / indigo somewhere (brand color #4F46E5), other colors are free

Use your image generation tool. Save the files in this folder as mascot-1.png through mascot-8.png. Then write choices.md with one line per mascot: the number, a short name, and one sentence describing it. Do not create or change any other files.
